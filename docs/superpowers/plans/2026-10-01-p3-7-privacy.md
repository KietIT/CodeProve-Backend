# P3.7 Privacy Basics Implementation Plan

> **For Claude:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:**
- Send OpenAI only what it needs.
- Ask students for consent before their work goes to an AI provider.
- Let them turn off AI personalisation.
- Publish a privacy policy the team (and a lawyer) has reviewed.

**Architecture:**
- **PII scrubbing:** every student-written message goes through one scrubber inside the shared mentor client, right before the API call, whatever feature sent it. The scrubber removes emails, phone numbers and the student's own full name.
- **`store=false`** is set explicitly on every OpenAI call.
- **Consent:** three new `users` columns record the consent time, the policy version, and the AI-personalisation choice.
- **Where consent is checked:** the LLM endpoints check consent. The learner brief (P3.5) is left out for students who opted out.
- **Policy:** the text is drafted here for review. The frontend pages are handed to CodeProve-UI.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy async, Alembic, pytest. No new dependency.

**What is already true:**
- No user id, name or email is sent to OpenAI: no `user` parameter, and the learner brief has no PII.
- Message text is never logged in `llm_calls`.
- The raters' export already scrubs emails and phone numbers.

**Legal note:** compliance with Vietnam's personal data decree (Nghị định 13/2023/NĐ-CP) needs a human legal review. This plan prepares the mechanisms and a draft; it is not legal advice.

## Decisions (approved by Kiệt 2026-10-01, decision 1 without student IDs)

1. **What the scrubber removes before any LLM call:**
   - emails;
   - Vietnamese phone numbers;
   - the student's own full name, when it appears whole (case-insensitive).
   Kiệt (2026-10-01): no student-ID pattern, because students are not only from FPT and ID formats differ. Partial names are not touched: removing a given name could change code or meaning. What is stored in our database stays as the student typed it; only what leaves for OpenAI is scrubbed.
2. **Consent is required for the AI features:**
   - Ciel, the hypothesis check, the explain-back questions and scoring return 403 `{code: "privacy_consent_required"}` until the student accepts the current policy version.
   - New email sign-ups accept it with a required checkbox.
   - Google sign-ups and existing accounts see a one-time consent dialog after login.
   - Everything else (the editor, running tests, Daily Bug Hunt, dashboard) works without consent.
3. **What "turn off AI personalisation" does:** it stops sending the learner brief to Ciel. The hint style stays, because it depends on the exercise level, not on the student. Recommendations and the progress page also stay: they are computed on our server and never sent to the AI. The toggle is on the Profile page and on by default after consent.
4. **Privacy policy:** I draft it (vi + en) in `docs/legal/chinh-sach-quyen-rieng-tu.md`. It covers:
   - what we collect and why;
   - that OpenAI (in the USA) processes the text, with `store=false`;
   - retention;
   - rights;
   - the contact email trinhkiet2005@gmail.com (given by Kiệt).
   The team reviews it (and a lawyer, if possible) before the frontend publishes it as version `2026-10`.
   The draft says that account deletion and data export are handled by email for now: the self-service API is P3.8, so the policy must not promise it yet.
